# A move retains nothing

Retention was unconditional, so a confirmed move filed the source item in the source collection's trash beside its copy in the target, and a restore would have duplicated it. STORAGE §11 now excepts an identity another collection of the same account holds live: the row is retired and purged in the same transaction, the holder pinning the body, the purge counting for the feed. GUIDE §5, §7 and §12 and OVERVIEW §7 follow. A delete with no holder elsewhere retains as before.
