# One standard, three documents, one status each

SPEC.md was the whole standard and it was the store. With the sync and search parts folded in it would have been three times its size and frozen as one, while the store is nearly stable and the other two are not. The standard is now SPEC.md, SYNC.md and SEARCH.md, each with its own status line, SPEC.md's preamble naming the three and README.md and AGENTS.md following.

A part freezes on its own; while a part is draft its schema is edited in place on the terms §6 already set.
