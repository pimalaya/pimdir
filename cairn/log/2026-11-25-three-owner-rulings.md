## Rules and their checks

- **SEARCH §7: a series ending before it starts has no instance at its start** (G28). A series every `RRULE` of which states an `UNTIL` whose date falls before the date its `DTSTART` states yields no occurrence but those its `RDATE`s and overrides name; an `UNTIL` earlier on the date of `DTSTART` cuts nothing. A stated departure from RFC 5545 §3.8.5.3, where `DTSTART` defines the first instance of the set: such a series is the shape a client leaves when it deletes a series from its first instance on, and search shows what Pimalaya's calendars, which expand with ical-rs (0.7.3 src/recur/set.rs `is_cut_before_start`), show. The date, not the instant, is ical-rs's reading and the one the question to the owner named ("on a day before its `DTSTART`"). For a person: a series deleted from its first instance on is not found by date, where the RFC's reading would bring it back as one ghost occurrence at its old start. OVERVIEW.md §10 follows. Check: vectors/search/extraction.json's new last case, "a series ending before it starts", over two new fixtures: event-cut-before-start.ics (weekly from 14 September, `UNTIL` 7 September, an `RDATE` on 23 September: no hit on the 14th, a hit on the 23rd, the text indexed) and event-until-same-day.ics (daily from 15:00 on 16 September, `UNTIL` 08:00 that day: a hit on the 16th, none on the 17th). Against io-pimdir built on an ical-rs counting `DTSTART` (`is_cut_before_start` answering false) the case fails on `kind:event date:2026-09-14`, and on one comparing instants rather than dates on `kind:event date:2026-09-16`; io-pimdir as it stands passes it. The case list is appended to, nothing else of the file moving.
- **STORAGE §5, §11.4: a release lets a message body's `shared_object` go** (G29). §11.4 now releases a binding's `base_object` and `shared_object` naming the body it frees, the body falling to the collector; §5 gains the exception to `shared_object` outliving the item's body: a message never diverges (SYNC §9), so no merge reads an ancestor of it (the reason cairn/log/2026-10-30-a-merge-keeps-every-body.md gave for release_bases_before). §14's "A body two members stating one hint would share" already named both and releases "as §11.4 releases one", so it reads alike now; §10's "pinned (§5)" carries the exception by reference. Check: no vector, since vectors/sync/ has no owner release verb and refresh.json's writes state no binding, so pinning it there would mean a new verb and a sync format for one rule; io-pimdir pins its side (tests/invariants_retention.rs `l1232_a_release_clears_only_the_released_shared_object`, `l1237_released_bodies_fall_to_the_collector_alone`, passing before and after) and its generated search suite pins the generator's: with G29's classifier gone it failed on the old generator's upkeep, which cleared the base alone ("the model does not follow the write" on `list_item_bindings` from each seed's release step on), and passes on the new one.
- **STORAGE §11.1: a revive carrying another body adopts it and releases the retained one.** The rule stood; its first sentence is tightened, since tasks.md "Left on 2026-11-20" had read it as keeping the old body: "**A revive adopts the incoming body when there is one**, and the retained body it replaces is released, kept apart for no kind." §11's list of kept bodies names the bodiless revive alone, as before, and SYNC §6 says the same. Check: vectors/sync/233, a card retained as Jane Doe revived by a member at `r2` carrying Jane A. Doe: the row holds the carried body at `Full`, based and agreed on it, `Added` alone, one item row. It passes on io-pimdir and fails on it with `revive_item` keeping the retained body whenever it differs from the carried one, as the generator's engine did ("events differ", an `Overruled` and a second, retained row).
