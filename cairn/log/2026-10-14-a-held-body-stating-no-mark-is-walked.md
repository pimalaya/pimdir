# A held body stating no mark is walked for its columns

linked-and-streamed-body-columns left a placement linked to a held body with its listing's mark wherever no row holding that body stated one. Every store filled before marks were read off bodies holds such rows, so once writers stop walking bodies themselves the new row would keep the guess for good, as before that change.

- **Annex A.1**: a row holding the body that states no mark leaves the placement's mark and invitation to a walk of the held body, for those columns only, no stand-in recorded (§14.3) and the held rows left as they are. The clause "a mark no such row states leaving the listing's" is gone. Only the mark triggers the walk: a `NULL` invitation is also what a body inviting to nothing states.
- **GUIDE**, upgrade step 2: the same of a link.

No schema, query or vector change: `held_mail_summary` already answers a row stating its mark first. Implemented in io-pimdir (`client::attachments::gaining`).
