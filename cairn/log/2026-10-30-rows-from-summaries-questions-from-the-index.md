# Rows from the summaries, questions from the index

From the Android app's calendar tab, slow to first paint: timed on a phone, a cold open of 478 entries spent 103 ms reading every body and 44 ms expanding every entry to show one row, and its Tasks and Contacts lists read and parse every body on each reload too. The user's decision (2026-10-30, breaking the drafts allowed): a summary holds what a list row shows, sorts by and what the store's references read; questions (text, calendar time, what a card holds) are the index's. The two places a list still needed a body are closed on that split: a task's capture time and waited-on person are row data, so they join the summaries; a card's presence filters and categories are questions, so the index records them. Dropping the summaries for the index was weighed and refused: an envelope-only message, a file and a bodiless placement's index row hold nothing else, the store's sort key and references derive from them, and the index is optional, lagging and rebuilt.
