# The generator's count and bytes profiles

Plan 2's count and bytes profiles, of cairn/changes/the-freeze-plan, for io-pimdir's scale tests (plan 3). No part of the standard moves.

- **The count profile** (generator/src/profile/count.rs): 100,000 to 200,000 small items in three accounts of 105 collections each: 100 mailboxes, a chain of them six deep, two address books and three calendars, one source per account and kind, each closed one round over everything. Eight items in ten are messages, one in ten cards, one in ten events, spread with a cubic skew; a tenth of the messages stay at `Meta`; one in a hundred is copied into the account's Archive under its `seq` (STORAGE §9.1). Every identity is fresh, so each is placed under its own key and a drawn `seq` (`Model::place`, `Model::fresh_seq`), the model never scanning what it holds per item. `profile::count(seed, items)` draws it at another size.
- **The bytes profile** (generator/src/profile/bytes.rs, generator/src/body.rs): 302 messages carrying an attachment, sized log-uniformly from 16 KiB to 4 MiB besides one of 26 MiB and one of 101 MiB. Each body is a `Stream`, the message's head, the base64 of lines of bytes drawn from its seed and its tail, named by digesting it as it renders and written by the writer the same way, never held whole (`Model::streams`, `Model::hold_stream`, `Model::object_size`); its row is derived as the held renderer derives it, `size` the stream's length. One in twenty stays at `Meta`; every twenty-fifth and the two largest are copied into the Archive sharing body and `seq`. `profile::bytes(seed, scale)` divides every size.
- **The oracle at scale** (generator/src/oracle.rs): `oracle::check_model` runs each expectation as it is stated, holding none but the last, its listings paged as the caller asks: a count store's expectations, all held, took more than 16 GB before the run was stopped. `list_link_placements` and `list_object_placements` are stated from one pass grouping the placed items, where a scan per key was quadratic; the writer groups the round stamps the same way.
- **Checks**: tests hold the count profile's shape (hundreds of collections, a mailbox six levels under its account, every kind, a `seq` one identity, a copy sharing its `seq`, the same seed giving the same store) and the bytes profile's (each stream rendering as many bytes as its size states and digesting to its name, each blob the writer wrote digesting to its name, the `size` column the body's length, a copy sharing its body); a store of each, at a size a test affords, reads back what its model states. Two ignored tests write and check each profile at its own size, passing in a release build (a count store checked with pages of 1,000 rows, pages of 100 leaving the merged mail listing and the searches to dominate). Broken by hand to see each fail: a stream's size off by its last line break, the `size` column not the stream's length, a copy drawing a `seq` of its own, the streams left unwritten.
- **Finding** (spec minor): no canonical statement writes `collections.parent`, nor `color`, `description` or `sort_order`, which every collection listing answers. The count profile's hierarchy is therefore its ids' paths alone.
