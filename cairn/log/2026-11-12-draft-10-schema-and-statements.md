# Draft 10: the schema and the statements

The first batch of the audit of 2026-11-12 (cairn/changes/audit-2026-11-12, phase 1): the schema and statement lines of its ledger, landed together with their checks. The three parts move to **storage-draft-10**, **sync-draft-10** and **search-draft-10** in their Status lines, README.md, AGENTS.md, `init_store_meta`, `init_index_meta`, conventions/gtd.md's requirement and the scenarios; the tags are the owner's to make. A store or an index a draft-09 program wrote is refused, as every draft's is. The payloads, the sync rules, the vectors and Annex A are untouched: later batches own them.

## Rules and their checks

Each line names the ledger id, the rule, where it is stated and the check that fails without it. Unless said otherwise the check is a scenario of checks/invariants.sh.

## Measurements

SQLite 3.50.4 (the flake's) and the 3.18.2 floor build, a scratch store built from migrations/storage/0001_init.sql on tmpfs, `synchronous` off, one transaction, user plus system time of the sqlite3 shell pinned to an idle core, eleven runs per variant, the variants alternating. They compare variants and hold no fsync cost.

- **The `GLOB` on `mail_summary.date`** (F4). 100,000 summaries inserted for 100,000 items already there: median 0.093 s with the `CHECK`, 0.077 s without (lowest 0.089 and 0.072), 21% more. The whole fill, the 100,000 items with their stamp triggers then their summaries: median 0.550 s with, 0.530 s without (lowest 0.544 and 0.527), 3.8% more. Above the 3% the batch allowed on either reading, so that one `CHECK` is dropped; the other shape `CHECK`s cost an insert nothing measurable (their columns are `NULL` or one row per source, queue row or receipt).
- **The guard on `changed`** (F5): `BEFORE UPDATE OF changed` on `items` and `collections`, refusing a value other than -1 or the next stamp. It breaks no stamp trigger (100,000 inserts drew 100,000 distinct stamps under it, and a direct write of a stamp was refused), but every stamp then fires it: the 100,000-item fill took a median 0.481 s with it and 0.464 s without (lowest 0.473 and 0.458), 3.7% more, and 3.6% more with the summaries. Above 3%, so it is not landed.
- **P3**: 60,001 items, 87,001 bindings, three sources. `collection_sources` on the largest collection: 11 to 12 ms before, under 1 ms after, on both SQLites; `list_sources`: 22 to 23 ms (33 to 53 ms on 3.18.2) before, under 1 ms after. The same rows before and after for five collections (two sources and a checkpoint alone, one source, a checkpoint alone, another kind, none) on both.
