A `Copy` meets none, tombstoning nothing. A reader's overlay folds nothing for such a `move`. Checks: sync vectors 155 (the binding's conflict) and 156 (the item's, through a binding that is not conflicted), each refused with the rows as they were; checks/vectors.py holds a `Move` to the rule it holds an `Edit` and a `Remove` to, and fails when no case moves over each conflict.

Checks: `collection:home/INBOX is:flagged` and `is:unread` complete, `subject:reunion`, `has:attachment` and `thread:multi-1@example.org` not, each re-derived by checks/vectors.py.
- **O6, a group read through, and an `@` in a text field** (SEARCH §8.2, §8.3, §8.5, §11; GUIDE §15). A group that is itself a conjunct of the whole query lends it its conjuncts, at any depth; a negated group, or one beside an `or`, rules no collection out. The value of a role field or of `with:` holding `@` is an address; in any other text field it is a character, so `subject:a@b` is a text term and may end with the wildcard. `person:` is not among the address fields, §8's table matching it on the title field. Checks: `(collection:home/Kept) is:read` and `((collection:home/Kept) (is:read))` with the coverage and the answer of the bare query, `-(collection:home/Kept) is:flagged` with the whole store's coverage, `collection:home/Lost subject:ledger@example.org` not complete; checks/vectors.py reads a group where it read none.
