# The generator's engine, sixth part: several sources

The sixth part of plan 2's "The scripted remote states", of cairn/changes/the-freeze-plan. No part of the standard moves.

- **The model** (generator/src/model.rs, writer.rs, oracle.rs): a collection holds the sources syncing it, each with its checkpoint and rounds, and its cross-source `conflict` policy, written by `set_conflict` and stated for `load_conflict`; an item holds one binding per source, in source order, every read listing bindings (`list_item_bindings`, `handle_for_link`, `list_sources`, the conflict and refusal reads) stating each.
- **Joining and offering** (generator/src/engine.rs, SYNC §3, §6, §9): a member of a source binding none of the live item holding its key joins it when its summary agrees, the flags folded with no base, an agreeing body taken as the base, the shared body standing for a message; a message disagreeing is minted apart (vector 93). An item a source lacks is offered as an `Add` under the provisional handle when the store holds its body and its key is not `hash:`, bound by the write recording an accepted outcome, or a refused one with its refusal (vector 171), and by nothing on a rejection.
- **Absorbing** (SYNC §9): a pull is settled by the source's policy first (§5), then folded: when the source changed, the item changed for that binding (against `shared_object`, falling back to the base) and the bodies differ, the collection's policy decides, `manual` (or any policy when a body is unread) recording the item conflict, which holds every binding's push; `prefer-incoming` and `prefer-existing` keep the body not adopted as a retained item over the member's handle, reported `Overruled`. `shared_object` moves only onto the body an unconflicted item settles on. A `PreferRemote` pull keeps the local body only when the item had not changed for the binding: a body another source folded is not that source's local edit. A server edit over an item conflict is recorded on its binding (vector 33). A delete one source finds tombstones the item while another binds it; that one pushes the `Remove` and the item is retained once bound by nobody (vectors 75, 76).
- **The drain** (generator/src/queue.rs, STORAGE §15.2, §15.3): an `update` or a `remove` stating `resolves` runs through the binding whose conflict it names, so a resolution naming the item's conflict and not that binding's parks.
- **A history** of four address books, one per cross-source policy plus one where both servers make the same edit, each through two first rounds, offers both ways, an edit one source folds, the other source's diverging or agreeing edit, a second edit over the result, and a delete propagated; and a mailbox where a message joins, another under its `Message-ID` is minted apart, and flags reach the other server.
- **Checks**: tests state each rule above from SYNC and STORAGE; every step's store is written and self-checked. Broken by hand to see each fail: a `hash:` item offered, no join, `prefer-incoming` read as `manual`, no divergence on a pull, an item conflict not holding pushes or not recording a server edit on the binding, a delete retaining while another source binds, `shared_object` moved by a conflicted upsert, a rejected offer left bound, a message's body diverging on joining, an agreeing pull diverging, a loser kept over an item another source changed, the join replacing flags rather than folding them, a resolution running through the first binding. The `PreferRemote` keep was the one defect the tests found.
