# The projection reads the item's conflict and its deletion, and a fetch moves the base

`items.conflicted` was recorded and read by nothing: under the `manual` cross-source policy the losing source still projected `Dirty` and pushed the shared body over its own remote, the conflict recorded after the damage, and nothing ever cleared the flag or released `items.conflict_object`. Rule 1 of SYNC §3 now projects `Conflict` for every binding of a conflicted item; an `Edit` or a `Remove` settles it (§7), and `list_conflicted_items` lists what waits, on the new partial index. Rule 3 requires `deleted = 0`, since a deleted item with a body was offered as a `Created` placement to every source lacking it and re-added on a third server. Rule 4's body clause is for mutable kinds only, and a placement with no body owes none: after a pull the other sources read the empty body as an edit to push.

SYNC §9's divergence test is stated as three facts read before the upsert, against the base the binding held and not the one the upsert carries, which is where the two-endpoint loss re-entered; `manual` keeps the shared body. An immutable kind never diverges, the binding's base adopting the shared body, so a mail fetched at `Full` beside a linked copy no longer sits `Dirty` for ever with nothing to push. A `Full` fetch over a placement with no local edit sets `base_object` and `base_revision`, which §6 had said only of the link path, so an engine reading it literally pushed every fetched body back; a landing sets the base to the fetched flags and, for an immutable kind, the staged body, which is what vectors 07 and 21 already showed. The §5 sentence keeping a create meeting a member as a conflict whatever the policy contradicted §6's landing and is gone: a mutable body differing from the staged one is the both-changed case. A `Conflict` outranked a `Tombstone`, so a conflicted contact could never be deleted; a `Remove` is now the decision, the base adopting the conflict's revision so the delete pushes against what the remote holds (vector 32). A fetched diverging body equal to the placement's own resolves the conflict, the lost push record having landed. Linking by `Message-ID` needs the summary's `size` to agree with the held body. Vectors 28 and 30 pin the divergence and the base move. SYNC §3, §5, §6, §7, §9; STORAGE §10, §14.1; GUIDE §9 to §12, §14.
