# SYNC binds where it used to describe

The part carried eight RFC 2119 words over eleven sections, most rules being declarative prose an implementer could read as description. Every rule now carries MUST or SHALL, and the head says a sentence without one describes. Four rules were sharpened on the way, each a divergence the reference engine showed: a content pull lowers the level to Probed; an accepted content push rebases the placement the flag merge wrote; a rekey meeting a revision its base never held carries a pull or a Conflict, never a base claiming it; a source bound beside others defaults to `Keep`.

Two contradictions with the engine were settled in its favour: a mutation of a probe is refused, replacing "an unresolved identity stages the source half alone"; a tombstone's flags ride along under §9. A sentence names the source-level and collection-level conflict policies as two axes.
