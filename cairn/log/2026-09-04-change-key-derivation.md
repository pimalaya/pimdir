# The idempotency key is derived by the part

SYNC §4 made the key normative in effect, a connector logging keys recognising a replay, and fixed no derivation, so two conforming engines could not recognise each other's. The derivation is now stated: FNV-1a 64 over NUL-terminated fields naming the collection, the handle, the kind and the state the change makes true, sixteen hex digits. Every push in vectors/sync/ carries its key and checks/vectors.py re-derives it from the prose.
