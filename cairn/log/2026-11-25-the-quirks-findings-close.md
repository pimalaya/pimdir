## Q1, for the owner: a server edit that keeps its ETag

SYNC §5 decides a pull by the revision alone: "A remote revision the base does not hold is a pull". A DAV server may serve another body under the ETag the base holds (quirks `revisions` step 2, every seed); a DAV listing carries bodies (SYNC §4), so the engine sees the change. The text says nothing of a carried body at the base's own revision. The generator's model keeps the old body and its summary; io-pimdir keeps the old body and writes the carried body's summary and sort key over it, a row disagreeing with itself under either reading.

- Reading A, the revision decides: the server's body is hidden until its ETag moves. A local edit pending over the card is pushed gated on that ETag, which the server accepts, and the server's edit is overwritten unseen: a silent loss.
- Reading B, a carried body differing from the base's at the base's revision is a remote edit, handled as a revision the base does not hold: pulled when nothing is pending, the both-changed case under the source's policy when a local edit is (under `Manual` a conflict a person settles). The server's body is shown; nothing is pushed over it unread.

Recommended: B. It is the only reading under which no policy pushes over a body nobody has read (SYNC §5), it costs a hash comparison of a body the listing already carries, and a listing carrying no body leaves the revision deciding, as now. It would move SYNC §5's content axis (and §8's carry, which reads the fetched revision the same way), a sync-draft-17 edited in place while sync-draft-16 is untagged, with a vector per case (pulled; conflicted over a pending edit), the generator's `refresh` and io-pimdir's `name_members` following. Until the ruling, io-pimdir tests/generated/campaign.rs files these differences under `ETAG_KEPT_OVER_A_SERVER_EDIT` so a campaign runs.
