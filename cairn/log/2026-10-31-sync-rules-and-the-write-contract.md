The three parts now state one convention: every statement of behaviour is normative, a key word marking its requirement level and a statement carrying none binding as one carrying MUST; notes, examples and rationale are informative and marked: a paragraph opening with *Note:*, an instance given in parentheses or after "for example", a reason given with "so", "since" or "because". SYNC's sentences that were descriptive on purpose are marked: the introduction's remark on the reference engine, the band head's misses, the three paragraphs of the unguarded window; two free-standing reasons (creates waiting for their page, a rekey dropping a gone handle first) became "since" clauses. Guarded by checks/names.sh, which requires the shared convention in each part's opening and refuses a key word in a *Note:* paragraph; the tree before failed it on all three parts.
- **A retained row revived without a body** (STORAGE §11, §11.1, §9; SYNC §5, §6; S2). A placement of a mutable kind carrying no body (a `Meta` listing or fetch, a bodiless `Copy` or `Move`) revives the row holding none, at `Meta`, for the upgrade to fetch, and the body the row held is kept as a retained item keyed over the reviving binding's handle and reported `Overruled`: kept as the item's, it differed from a base naming no body, projected `Dirty` and was pushed over the server's current body. An immutable kind keeps the retained body as its base, as before, now only when the row agrees on the size too where both state one, its body becoming the member's unread as a link's does. Guarded by vectors/sync/108 (vector 61's end state relisted at e2 at `Meta`: no push, the item at `Meta`, n1 retained under `dup:00000042oc1nst4nce#00000042oc1nst4nce`), 109 (a retained message relisted under its `Message-ID` with another subject, sender and date: minted) and 110 (the same subject, date and sender, another size: minted). checks/vectors.py gains a check that no binding whose base names a revision and no body faces a live, unconflicted item holding a body, one that an `Update` gated on another revision than the binding's stored base is gated on a body the run read, and the agreement check now reads every held row, a retained one on its size too; its `Overruled` key check finds the binding a revive creates. - **No policy settles against a body nobody read** (SYNC §5, §6, §9; STORAGE §10; S3). - **`PreferLocal` keeps the body it pushes over** (SYNC §5, §9; the open item of 2026-10-30-an-automatic-settle-keeps-what-it-discards, closed). In a write that commits before the push, the binding's base adopts the observed revision and the member's body and that body is kept as a retained item, reported `Overruled`; the local body is then pushed gated on that revision. The keep lands once: a push rejected or replayed finds the base already moved, so no divergence is measured again, and one that landed unrecorded is read back as the push whose record was lost. Guarded by vectors/sync/117 (the member carrying c3, `carries`: c3 retained, c2 pushed gated on r2, the base on c2 at r3) and vectors.py's check that a push over a body the run read keeps it; seed: no keep (refused). That the keep commits before the push is engine behaviour the vector format, which states no write order, cannot observe.
- **A narrowed listing proves only what its filter could list** (SYNC §4, §5; STORAGE §10; S4). Such a round finds absent only a member dated before the round opened; an undated or later-dated one only when the enumeration lacks it, else it is left as it stands. `list_unstamped_bindings` takes `:narrowed`, `:enumerated` and the `handles` slot. Guarded by invariants.sh, "narrowed: …" (three scenarios; the two binding `:narrowed` 1 failed before, the statement ignoring them), by vectors/sync/107 (Graph's narrowed round keeps an undated draft and a 2035-dated message, drops the 5 October one) and vector 47, rewritten (narrowed, the enumeration lacking 11, so 11 still drops), and vectors.py's absence rule; seeds: 107 dropping 11 and 13, 47 without its enumeration (both refused).

- **The producer's gate is advice; the owner's backstop decides** (STORAGE §15, §15.6, Annex B.1; D8, S9).
