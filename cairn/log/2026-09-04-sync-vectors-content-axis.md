# The sync vectors reach the content axis

Ten vectors, all immutable mail with no revision, pinned none of §5's content axis, no conflict policy, no add outcome, no delta, no move and only the trivial rekey, so an engine could pass every one and disagree on most of the part. Fourteen cases join them: a content pull and push, the four policies including KeepBoth's provisional handle, an accepted and a rejected add, a vanished delta, a relocating move, a landed arrival, a minting rekey and a rekey meeting a remote edit twice. card-basic-edited.vcf is the second body of one identity they need.

§11 now says what a push is compared on, key, precondition, origin and object included, and how an outcome carries an assigned handle and a revision, so a future vector can pin what the first ten could not.
