## Findings

- **o06-L476-s1** (also o06-L473-s3, o09-L591-s1). Base §6: "under `prefer-incoming` the item adopts it, and under `prefer-existing` keeps its own, the conflict cleared either way and the body not adopted kept and reported (§9), when the item holds a body; one holding none waits, recorded as `manual` records it, for the fill that reads its own (§9)." Rewrite §9, table B, row "the missing body read, differing": `manual` cell "the policy applies as to a carried body, when the item holds a body, one holding none waiting, recorded as `manual` records it, for the fill that reads its own: `manual` keeps the conflict, and a fetched incoming body MUST land in `items.conflict_object`, never in the item's own object"; `prefer-incoming` cell "adopts the incoming body, the conflict cleared"; `prefer-existing` cell "keeps the item's, the conflict cleared". The condition the base puts on the two `prefer-*` policies (only when the item holds a body, else wait recorded as `manual`) now sits in the `manual` cell, and the `prefer-*` cells read unconditionally. Read by column, `prefer-incoming` adopts a fetched incoming body over an item that holds none, and `prefer-existing` "keeps" a body the item does not hold, instead of waiting for the fill. Severity: changed.

- **o07-L492-s1** (target o05-L357-s1). Base §7: "A diverging body not fetched yet (`conflict_object` `NULL`) is named by no resolution, so no `Remove` settles that conflict before the upgrade has read it (§6), and a base never pairs the observed revision with the old body." Rewrite §5: "No policy pushes over a body nobody has read, and no base pairs the observed revision with the old body." In the base the clause follows "so": by the preamble it is an informative consequence of the resolution rule for a `Remove`. The rewrite states it as a bare, unmarked clause, so it now binds as MUST, and over every write of a base in every verb (pull, policy, landing, rekey), not as a consequence of one `Remove` rule. Nothing in the base contradicts it, but the base never required it as a rule of its own. Severity: added.

- **o05-L384-s2**. Base: "One whose relocation stated none is listed by the target's next listing under a new handle, and the page naming it lands the create (§6); until then the create waits, as every create waits for the page that lands it." Rewrite: "One whose relocation stated none is listed by the target's next listing under a new handle, and waits, as every create waits, for the page naming it to land the create (§6)." The base's positive "the page naming it lands the create" is now only the object of a wait, and the subject of "waits" is now the relocated member ("One"), not the create. §6 still requires the landing, so nothing binding is lost. Severity: wording.

- **o06-L473-s2**. Base §6: "A body fetched equal to the item's own is no divergence, whatever the policy: the item's conflict clears and the binding takes that body as its base and its `shared_object`, as a binding's own conflict clears on its own body (§5)." Rewrite §6: "An item conflict holding no `conflict_object`, recorded before its incoming body was read, is revisited by the upgrade and settled by the collection's policy as §9 says (table B)." Table B, row "the missing body read, equal to the other": "no divergence, whatever the policy: the conflict clears (§6), and the binding whose upgrade fetched it takes that body as its base and its `shared_object`". The meaning is kept, but "the item's conflict" became "the conflict" (in a table whose `manual` and policy rows also concern binding conflicts), and "(§6)" now points to the §6 sentence that only points back to table B: a circular reference. Severity: wording.

- **o05-L340-s1** (with o05-L355's "A placement holding no body has nothing to keep and pulls as any"). Base: "A placement holding no body (a pull having emptied it) owes nothing until the upgrade refetches it." Rewrite table A, last row: "| no body held | owes nothing (above) | same | same | pulls as any (above) |". The row sets "owes nothing" (the push side) against "pulls as any" (the pull side) as if they were alternatives. Read by column, the `KeepLocal` cell no longer says the placement owes nothing, and the other three no longer say it pulls. The prose rule in §5 ("owes nothing, whatever its base names, until the upgrade refetches it") still binds every policy, so the meaning survives. Severity: wording.

- **o04-L214-s1**. Base: "Each can pass with the change as it stands (the network back, the credential renewed, the lock released, space freed), where a change held by its key (§5) would never be pushed again."
